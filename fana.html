The number in the box is the bout number — where that entry sits in the pool’s running order. It is the difference between going on at the start and waiting two hours, so the dashed line shows where that lands: pool window divided by the number of bouts, counted from the top. Sparring runs about 5 minutes a bout, patterns about 2¾, technique events about 3½ per competitor.

Treat the estimate as a guide, not a call time. It assumes the ring runs to schedule and holds its order; walkovers pull it earlier and anything long pushes it later. The bout number itself is exact.

Colour is the corner the draw puts them in for that first bout: in every pairing the upper slot is stroked red and the lower blue, and a win means a fresh colour next round. Special Technique and Power Breaking have no corner — there the number is the competitor order.
